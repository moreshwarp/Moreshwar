Research notes / methodology

From a question to a test

Start with a falsifiable hypothesis. Identify assumptions, choose a measurable outcome, compare against a baseline, and disclose uncertainty. A plausible explanation is not evidence by itself.

  1. State the question and competing explanations
  2. Choose what can be measured
  3. Design a small reproducible experiment
  4. Report limitations alongside the result
