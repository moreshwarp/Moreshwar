Reading notes

Paul Graham: reading notes

Independent explanations of ideas from Paul Graham's essays, written for my own thinking and kept here in case they help someone else. These are interpretations, not reproductions of the essays.

The archive I worked from lists 20 essay titles but carries full text for only two. Those two have notes below; the rest are listed for reference only. Dates in that archive are compilation dates, not original publication dates.

01

Available reading notes

Each note states the idea, then the part I find useful in engineering and research work.

Schlep Blindness

Why avoiding unpleasant work can hide the most valuable problems, and how to separate how tedious a problem is from how much it matters.

 Maker's Schedule, Manager's Schedule

Why focused work and meeting-heavy days pull in opposite directions, and what a single interruption really costs.
