Paul Graham: reading notes

A curated collection of independent explanations and practical reflections on Paul Graham's essays. The uploaded archive contains a list of 20 essay titles, but full commentary for only two. The remaining titles are listed for reference; their text has not been supplied.

These are interpretations, not reproductions of Graham's essays. Dates in the supplied PDF are archive compilation dates, not original publication dates.
