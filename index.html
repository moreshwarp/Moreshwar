Hello!

I'm Moreshwar. I am interested in systems software, distributed computing, reliability, and the methods used to test claims about computer systems.

This website is my independent research notebook. It contains questions I am investigating, small educational experiments, and essays that document what I learn. Work in progress is identified as such.

My aim is to understand how real systems behave, especially when concurrency, failures and imperfect information make simple explanations unreliable.

Research interests and experiments

These are exploratory learning projects, not claims of peer-reviewed discoveries or published benchmarks.

  • Distributed systems and reliability — coordination, queueing, latency, failure behaviour, and consistency models.
  • Research methodology — hypotheses, baselines, reproducibility, and testing competing explanations.
  • Scientific and mathematical foundations — models, proof, uncertainty, and reasoning about software.

About

I use this site to document questions, reason from evidence, and build reproducible examples. Interests include distributed systems, operating systems, concurrency, mathematical foundations and careful technical communication.

Writing here reflects independent exploration. It should not be interpreted as a record of formal academic appointments, peer-reviewed publications, or proven scientific breakthroughs.
