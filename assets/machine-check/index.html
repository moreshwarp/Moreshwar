What are we testing?

The check samples screen-update timing for five seconds. Long waits can happen when the browser is busy, the page is hidden, or the computer is under load.

Run it again under similar conditions to compare. One short sample is a clue, not a diagnosis.

How to read these signals

Every number has a source and a limit. Here is the short version.

Response timeLong gaps mean this tab did not get a screen update on time. It is not a direct CPU-speed test.
Processor countHow many logical processors the browser reports. It does not show current CPU load or exact chip speed.
MemoryA rounded device-memory estimate on supported browsers. It is not live RAM use or total installed RAM.
Site storageSpace used by this website in this browser. It is not free space on your physical drive.
Power & networkOptional browser hints. Battery percent and online status do not measure power draw, Wi‑Fi quality, or internet speed.
